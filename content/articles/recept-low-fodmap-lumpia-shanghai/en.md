# Recipe: Low-FODMAP Lumpia Shanghai

No Filipino party is complete without lumpia! These crispy Lumpia Shanghai leave out the garlic and onion, but not the flavour. Ginger, spring onion greens and garlic-infused oil do the heavy lifting. *Sarap!*

> **Please note:** this article is general information, not personal medical advice. If you have gut symptoms, talk to your GP or a FODMAP-trained dietitian.

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 400 g minced pork (or half chicken, half pork)
- 80 g carrot, finely grated (1 medium)
- 60 g white or Chinese cabbage (very finely shredded)
- 20 g spring onion greens, finely sliced (green part of 4 spring onions)
- 6 g fresh ginger, grated (1 tbsp)
- 14 g garlic-infused oil (1 tbsp)
- 24 g soy sauce or gluten-free tamari (1½ tbsp)
- 50 g egg (1, approx. 50 g without shell)
- 3 g salt (½ tsp)
- 1 g black pepper, ground (½ tsp, to taste)
- 6 g patis (fish sauce) (1 tsp; optional)

### Wrappers and frying

- 190 g rice paper or gluten-free spring roll wrappers (about 24 sheets of 22 cm; weight per sheet varies by brand)
- 500 ml lukewarm water, for soaking the rice paper (approx. 500 g)
- 920 g sunflower oil, for deep-frying (approx. 1 l; for the air fryer 14 g, 1 tbsp, for brushing is enough)

### Homemade chilli sauce

- 60 ml rice vinegar (approx. 60 g; 4 tbsp)
- 24 g sugar (2 tbsp)
- 2 g chilli flakes (1 tsp)
- 0.5 g salt (pinch)
- 3 g cornflour (1 tsp)
- 15 ml cold water (approx. 15 g; 1 tbsp)

### Toyo-calamansi

- 32 g soy sauce or gluten-free tamari (2 tbsp)
- 10 ml calamansi juice (approx. 10 g; 2 tsp; or lemon or lime juice)

## Method

1. **Squeeze the vegetables.** Squeeze the grated carrot and shredded cabbage well in a clean tea towel, so the filling isn't watery and the wrappers don't tear.
2. **Mix.** Mix the mince with the carrot, cabbage, spring onion greens, ginger, garlic-infused oil, 24 g soy sauce (1½ tbsp), the egg, salt, pepper and the patis if using.
3. **Fry a test ball.** Fry a small ball of filling (about 5 g) in a dry frying pan over medium-high heat for 1 to 2 minutes and taste. Adjust the salt or pepper before you start rolling.
4. **Roll.** Dip a sheet of rice paper in the lukewarm water for 3 to 5 seconds (don't soak spring roll wrappers). Place about 25 g of filling (a generous 1½ tbsp) as a thin log along the bottom, fold in the sides and roll up tightly. Repeat until the filling is used up (about 24 rolls).
5. **Make the chilli sauce.** Heat the rice vinegar, sugar, chilli flakes and the pinch of salt in a small saucepan over medium-high heat until the sugar dissolves, about 2 minutes. Mix the cornflour into the cold water, add it and stir for 1 minute until thickened. Leave to cool.
6. **Make the toyo-calamansi.** Stir 32 g soy sauce (2 tbsp) and the calamansi juice together in a small bowl.
7. **Deep-fry or air-fry.** Heat the sunflower oil to 170 to 175 °C and fry the lumpia in batches for 4 to 5 minutes until golden. Drain on kitchen paper. Or brush them with 14 g oil (1 tbsp) and cook in the air fryer at 190 °C for 12 to 14 minutes, turning halfway. Serve with both dipping sauces.

## FODMAP check

### Garlic-infused oil — *Low FODMAP*
Garlic-infused oil gives garlic flavour without fructans, because fructans don't dissolve in oil (Monash).

### Spring onion greens — *Low FODMAP*
Use only the green part of the spring onions; the white part is high FODMAP (Monash).

### Cabbage — *Low FODMAP*
Common (white) cabbage is on Monash's low-FODMAP shopping list; we have not checked separate Monash data for Chinese cabbage. Here it is 15 g of cabbage per person, a small serve.

### Carrot — *Low FODMAP*
Carrot is on Monash's low-FODMAP shopping list. Here it is 20 g per person.

### Soy sauce — *Low FODMAP*
Soy sauce is low FODMAP in a limited serve; check the Monash app for the current serving size. The filling and the toyo dip together come to about 14 g per person, less than 1 tbsp.

### Rice paper instead of wheat wrappers — *Swap*
Wheat lumpia wrappers contain fructans, so we avoid them during the elimination phase. Monash itself uses rice paper in recipes such as rice paper rolls. We have not checked a maximum number of sheets per serving with Monash; here it is about 6 per person.

### Patis and calamansi — *Uncertain*
We could not check patis (fish sauce) or calamansi with Monash. Patis is optional; lemon or lime juice is an alternative to calamansi.

### Shop-bought sweet chilli — *Avoid*
Many shop-bought chilli sauces contain garlic. Making your own takes five minutes.

## Gomez's tip: freeze them raw

Roll a double batch and freeze the raw lumpia spaced apart on a baking tray. Then they can go into a bag together. That way you always have a gut-friendly merienda ready. Cook them straight from the freezer, a little longer than fresh lumpia.
